Standard operating procedure · Service

Opening line check.

A 12-minute pass through the line before doors. Manager-on-duty signs off in Le Répertoire — anomalies escalate to the venue lead.

"Mise en place is the difference between a service and a scramble."
  1. Walk the pass. Confirm tickets, garnish, and pickup zone are clear and labelled.
  2. Temp-check all four reach-ins. Log to Compliance if any reading is >5°C.
  3. Confirm allergens board matches today's specials in the POS.
  4. Sign off in the app — your name + time stamps the line.
