### ✅ Developer Notes

* **Entity Structure:**

  * `single_venue` → A business with one location only
  * `multi_venue` → A business with multiple venues under a single company
  * `parent_company` → A parent organization owning multiple companies/venues across regions

* **Cross-Collection Links:**

  * `admin_user_id` → links to `business_users.linking_id`
  * `venue_manager_id` → links to `business_users.linking_id`
  * `work_area_id` → used for operational mapping (e.g., rostering, payroll segmentation)

* **Recommended Indexes:**
