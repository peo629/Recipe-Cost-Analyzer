Cigar of Smoked Salmon with Caviar

Primary Ingredient: Salmon
Cuisine: French

Ingredients
	•	Avurga caviar — 100g
	•	Egg whites — 1
	•	Icing sugar — 70g
	•	Plain flour — 140g
	•	Unsalted butter, melted — 100g
	•	Salt — 2 tsp
	•	Noilly Prat — 100ml
	•	Smoked salmon — 140g
	•	Cream, lightly whipped — 200ml

Instructions
	1.	Place the egg whites and icing sugar in a bowl and mix with whisk until smooth. Add the flour and mix until smooth hen whisk in the melted butter and the salt. Allow the mixture to rest for 2 hours until cold in the fridge.
	2.	Heat the oven to 200°C.
	3.	Using a small palette knife, spread a small amount of the tuille mixture onto the silk mat to form a neat 5 cm square, about 2 mm thick. Repeat to make 20 squares. Bake for 5 minutes or until slightly golden. Remove from the oven and while very hot, remove the squares from one at a time and wrap each one around the end of the wooden spoon, to create a cigar shape then repeat with all squares. Set aside to cool in a dry place.
	4.	For the smoked salmon mousse, heat the Noilly Prat in a small saucepan over high heat until reduced by half (you should have about a tablespoon). Place in a robot coupe with the smoked salmon and blend until smooth. Pass the mixture through a fine sieve into a bowl, and then fold through the whipped cream.
	5.	Fill a piping bag with a 0.5mm nozzle with the salmon mousse and pipe into the cigars. Slightly push in the ends and hen pipe in caviar to both ends. Serve.

Notes
	•	Adjust the seasoning and spice level according to your taste preferences.
	•	Serve the salt and pepper prawns hot, as an appetizer or main dish.
	•	You can also garnish with sliced chilies or a dipping sauce like sweet chili sauce or lemon wedges.
	•	If you don’t have a deep fryer, you can shallow fry the prawns in a pan with enough oil to cover them halfway.

Pork and Duck Rillette

Primary Ingredient: Pork
Cuisine: French

Ingredients

	•	Braised pork belly (shredded meat) — 500g
	•	Chicken jus — 200ml
	•	Duck fat — 150ml
	•	Shallot dressing — 200ml
	•	Confit garlic puree — 8g
	•	Chopped parsley — 5g
	•	Chopped tarragon — 5g
	•	Salt and pepper — null

Instructions

Simple Country Terrine

Ingredients
	•	25 slices of speck (to line the terrine)
	•	800g pork shoulder
	•	600g chicken breast
	•	400g chicken liver, minced
	•	600g pork fat, diced and minced
	•	1 garlic clove, crushed
	•	1 tsp four-spice powder
	•	1 tsp black pepper
	•	100ml cognac (or brandy)
	•	2 bunches of tarragon
	•	36g salt
	•	1 cup parsley, chopped
	•	8 sage leaves
	•	8 sprigs of thyme, leaves removed
	•	2 eggs, lightly beaten

Instructions
	1.	Line a terrine mold with the slices of speck.
	2.	In a large bowl, combine the pork shoulder, chicken breast, minced chicken liver, minced pork fat, crushed garlic, four-spice powder, black pepper, cognac (or brandy), tarragon, and salt. Mix well and marinate overnight in the refrigerator.
	3.	The next day, add the chopped parsley, sage leaves, and thyme leaves to the marinated mixture. Mix well.
	4.	Add the lightly beaten eggs and stir to combine.
	5.	Pack the terrine mixture into the lined terrine mold, pressing down to remove any air pockets.
	6.	Cover the terrine with a lid or aluminum foil.
	7.	Prepare a bain-marie (water bath) in a deep oven tray or roasting pan.
	8.	Place the covered terrine in the bain-marie and transfer to the preheated oven.
	9.	Bake at 160°C (320°F) for 1 hour, then reduce the temperature to 140°C (285°F) and continue baking until the internal temperature of the terrine reaches 70-72°C (158-162°F), which may take several hours.
	10.	Remove the terrine from the oven and allow it to cool completely before unmolding and slicing.
	11.	Serve the terrine chilled or at room temperature, with crusty bread, mustards, pickles, or other desired accompaniments.

Notes
	•	The terrine can be made a few days in advance and will keep refrigerated for several days.
	•	Adjust seasoning and ingredient quantities according to your taste preferences.
	•	For a smoother texture, you can pass the mixture through a meat grinder before baking.

Steak Tartare

Restaurant: Bistro Guillaume

Ingredients

Main
	•	200g beef tenderloin
	•	150g cornichons, diced
	•	150g shallots, diced
	•	50g capers, diced
	•	Chopped parsley
	•	Egg yolks

Garnish
	•	200g large russet potatoes
	•	50ml baby herbs
	•	Shallot vinaigrette

Dressing
	•	1 kitchen spoon Dijon mustard
	•	100ml cognac
	•	1 lt tomato sauce
	•	100ml Worcestershire sauce
	•	2 tsp Tabasco
	•	Salt & pepper

Instructions
	1.	Peel the potatoes and, using a lattice blade on a mandolin, slice the potatoes thinly.
	2.	Deep fry the potato slices until golden brown and set aside.
	3.	Dress the baby herbs with the shallot vinaigrette and set aside.
	4.	In a bowl, mix the beef tenderloin, diced cornichons, diced shallots, diced capers, chopped parsley, and egg yolks.
	5.	Prepare the tartare dressing by combining the Dijon mustard, cognac, tomato sauce, Worcestershire sauce, Tabasco, and salt & pepper to taste.
	6.	Add the desired amount of tartare dressing to the beef mixture and mix well. Season with additional salt and pepper to taste.
	7.	To serve, quenelle (scoop) the steak tartare onto a plate, and arrange the fried potato slices and dressed baby herbs on the side.

Notes
	•	Adjust the quantities of the ingredients according to your desired portion size.
	•	Serve the steak tartare with additional accompaniments like bread or crackers, if desired.
	•	For food safety reasons, use fresh, high-quality beef tenderloin for the tartare.

Snail Butter

Ingredients
	•	1.5kg unsalted butter, softened
	•	30g salt
	•	50g lemon juice
	•	25g garlic
	•	Parsley puree (see instructions)

Instructions

parsleyPuree
	1.	Pick fresh parsley and blend it in a Thermomix until it becomes a paste.
	2.	Place the parsley paste in Pacojet containers and freeze.
	3.	Run the frozen parsley through the Pacojet three times, freezing in between each run. This will create a smooth, fine parsley puree.

snailButter
	1.	Place the softened butter in a Hobart mixer with a paddle attachment.
	2.	On a slow speed, mix the butter until smooth and creamy.
	3.	Add the salt, lemon juice, and garlic to the butter while mixing, ensuring they are evenly dispersed throughout the butter.
	4.	Remove the butter from the mixer and add the desired amount of parsley puree to achieve the correct color and flavor.
	5.	Mix the parsley puree into the butter until well combined.

Notes
	•	Adjust the amount of parsley puree according to your desired flavor and color intensity.
	•	The snail butter can be used as a compound butter for grilling, sautéing, or garnishing dishes.
	•	Store the snail butter in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to a week or in the freezer for longer storage.
	•	Allow the butter to soften at room temperature before using, if necessary.

Salt and Pepper Prawns

Yield: Serves 20

Ingredients
	•	1 tsp Sichuan pepper
	•	1 tsp sea salt
	•	100g plain flour
	•	2 egg whites
	•	20 crystal bay prawns
	•	1 bunch coriander, trimmed and washed

Instructions
	1.	Preheat the oven to 170°C (338°F).
	2.	Place the Sichuan pepper on a baking tray and roast for 5 minutes or until very fragrant.
	3.	In a mortar and pestle, grind the roasted Sichuan pepper and sea salt until very fine.
	4.	Sieve the salt and pepper mixture together with the flour into a large bowl.
	5.	In a separate large bowl, whisk the egg whites until frothy.
	6.	Dip the prawns into the bowl of egg whites to coat using your hands, then into the bowl of flour to coat. Shake off any excess flour.
	7.	Preheat a deep fryer to 170°C (338°F).
	8.	Deep-fry the prawns for 1 minute, stirring continuously until golden brown, then remove with a spider and drain on paper towels.
	9.	Arrange the coriander on a serving plate.
	10.	Place the fried prawns on top of the coriander and serve immediately.

Notes
	•	Adjust the seasoning and spice level according to your taste preferences.
	•	Serve the salt and pepper prawns hot, as an appetizer or main dish.
	•	You can also garnish with sliced chilies or a dipping sauce like sweet chili sauce or lemon wedges.
	•	If you don’t have a deep fryer, you can shallow fry the prawns in a pan with enough oil to cover them halfway.

Caesar Dressing

Ingredients
	•	6 eggs (hard-boiled for 6 minutes from a cold start)
	•	1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
	•	2 Ortiz anchovies
	•	350ml vegetable oil
	•	1 teaspoon garlic puree
	•	300ml vinegar
	•	20ml lemon juice
	•	Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions
	1.	Hard-boil the eggs for 6 minutes from a cold start. Once cooked, peel and set aside.
	2.	In a blender, place the hard-boiled eggs, Dijon mustard, anchovies, garlic puree, and vinegar.
	3.	With the blender running, slowly drizzle in the vegetable oil, just like making mayonnaise. The mixture should thicken as the oil emulsifies.
	4.	Once the desired consistency is achieved, season the dressing with salt and pepper to taste.
	5.	Finally, add the lemon juice and pulse the blender a few times to incorporate it into the dressing.
	6.	Transfer the Caesar dressing to an airtight container and refrigerate until ready to use.

Notes
	•	For a smoother texture, you can pass the dressing through a fine-mesh sieve after blending.
	•	Adjust the amount of vinegar, lemon juice, and seasoning according to your taste preferences.
	•	Use the dressing to toss with crisp romaine lettuce, croutons, and freshly grated Parmesan cheese for a classic Caesar salad.
	•	The dressing can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.

Western Australian Blue Swimmer Crab Salad

Description: Fresh crab meat on a bed of avocado, with a coriander and capsicum coulis.
Cuisine: Australian
Prep Time: 45 minutes
Servings: 10
Tags: Seafood, Salad, Appetizer

Ingredients
	•	Coriander — 1 bunch, leaves only
	•	Telegraph cucumbers — 1, sliced into 1 mm thick slices
	•	Blue swimmer crab meat — 800 g, no shell
	•	Shallot vinaigrette — 125 ml
	•	Avocados — 5, halved and sliced vertically into 3 mm thick pieces
	•	Baby herbs — 3 pun
	•	Red capsicums (peppers) — 2, roasted, peeled and chopped roughly
	•	Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
	•	Sherry vinegar — 1 tablespoon
	•	Avocado — 1
	•	Milk — 30 ml
	•	Lime — 1/2

Instructions
	1.	For the capsicum coulis, place the capsicum and sherry vinegar in a blender, season with salt and pepper, blend until smooth. With the motor running, slowly add the olive oil and blend until emulsified. Set aside in a squeezy bottle.
	2.	For the avocado mousse, peel avocados and place all ingredients into a blender and blend until smooth. Set aside in a squeezy bottle.
	3.	Finely chop 3/4 of the coriander leaves. Blanch the cucumber in boiling water for 5 seconds, then refresh in a bowl of iced water.
	4.	Place the crab meat, finely chopped coriander, and 100 ml of the shallot dressing in a bowl and mix until combined.
	5.	To serve, divide the avocado slices among four plates, and arrange them in a single layer, in a circular shape, using 10 6 cm ring moulds placed in the avocados. Place the crab mixture on top of the avocado, then arrange the cucumber on top in a single layer, overlapping slightly.
	6.	Brush the cucumber with the remaining shallot dressing, and season.
	7.	Place drops of avocado mousse and capsicum coulis around the salad, and place baby herbs on top.

Traditional Onion Soup with Gruyère Crouton

Description: A classic French onion soup topped with a crispy gruyère cheese crouton.
Cuisine: French
Prep Time: 1 hour
Cook Time: 45 minutes
Servings: 25
Tags: Soup, Vegetarian, French

Ingredients
	•	Brown onions — 20kg
	•	Garlic cloves — 750ml
	•	Port — 750ml
	•	White wine — 10lt
	•	Thyme — 1 bunch
	•	Bay leaves — 400gm
	•	Veal stock — 00
	•	Chives — 300ml, chopped
	•	Baguettes — (for croutons)
	•	Gruyère cheese — (for croutons)
	•	Egg yolks — (for liaison)
	•	Cream — (for liaison)

Instructions
	1.	Brown onions in a brat pan with minimal oil. Onions will be very dark brown when ready.
	2.	Microplane garlic cloves and sweat out with onions.
	3.	Deglaze with port and wine and cook out alcohol.
	4.	Add veal stock, then thyme and bay leaf. Bring to a simmer and cook for 45 minutes.
	5.	Season with salt and pepper.
	6.	For the liaison: Whip cream till soft peak, then add egg yolks. Once combined, place in a container and store in the fridge.
	7.	For the croutons: Slice baguettes on an angle and deep fry until golden brown and crisp. Put approximately 5gms of grated gruyère on each and gratinate under a salamander.
	8.	To serve: Pour the liaison into bowls evenly (small amount). Ladle soup on top and finish with chopped chives.

Gratin Dauphinois

Description: A classic French potato gratin dish with cream, cheese, and garlic.
Cuisine: French
Prep Time: 30 minutes
Cook Time: 1 hour
Servings: 4
Tags: Potato, Gratin, Side Dish, Vegetarian

Ingredients
	•	Full cream milk — 500ml
	•	Double cream — 250ml
	•	Gruyère cheese — 140g, grated
	•	Salt — to season
	•	Pepper — to season
	•	Nutmeg — to season
	•	Desiree potatoes — 1kg, peeled and thinly sliced
	•	Garlic clove — 1, cut in half
	•	Unsalted butter — 50g, cut into small pieces

Instructions
	1.	Preheat oven to 190°C.
	2.	Bring milk to a boil over medium heat.
	3.	Add the cream and 3/4 of the grated cheese, and stir to combine.
	4.	Season with salt, pepper, and nutmeg to taste, then add the sliced potatoes.
	5.	Cook until the potatoes are soft, around 20-30 minutes.
	6.	Prepare a 3-liter oven-proof baking dish by rubbing the inside with the halved garlic clove.
	7.	Pour the potato and liquid mixture into the prepared dish.
	8.	Sprinkle the remaining grated cheese and diced butter over the top.
	9.	Place in the oven and bake until the potatoes are cooked through and the top is golden brown.

Pea and Ham Soup with Gruyère Beignets

Description: A creamy pea soup with crispy gruyère cheese beignets and a speck lardons garnish.
Cuisine: French
Prep Time: 45 minutes
Cook Time: 30 minutes
Servings: 4
Tags: Soup, Peas, Beignets, French

Ingredients
	•	Frozen peas — 2kg
	•	White onion — 1, finely sliced
	•	Garlic cloves — 2, finely sliced
	•	Milk — 750ml
	•	Vegetable stock — 750ml
	•	Butter — 100g
	•	Extra virgin olive oil — 2 tsp
	•	Sea salt — to taste
	•	Boiling water — 200ml
	•	Unsalted butter — 75g
	•	Plain flour — 100g
	•	Eggs — 2
	•	Gruyère cheese — 75g, grated
	•	Paprika — 1 tsp
	•	Dijon mustard — 1 tsp
	•	Speck lardons — for garnish
	•	Cav vinegar — for garnish
	•	Beurre noisette — for garnish
	•	Thyme leaves — for garnish

Instructions
	1.	For the green pea purée: Sweat garlic and onion in butter and olive oil with salt until tender, no color. Pour hot milk over and hot stock, bring to a boil. Add peas, return to a boil, quickly strain, keeping all the liquid. Blend in a blender, gradually adding stock to bring back to consistency, season, pass through a drum sieve over a bowl of ice to preserve the color.
	2.	For the gruyère beignets: Add the butter to a pan of boiling water. Once the butter has melted, add flour and whisk the mixture quickly with a wooden spoon until it comes away from the sides of the pan and has a smooth texture. Allow the mixture to cool for 5 minutes, then add eggs, cheese, mustard, and paprika, stirring well between each addition. Using a teaspoon dipped in vegetable oil, place spoonfuls of the rounded mixture into the fryer until golden brown and puffed; they should float to the surface of the oil when ready.
	3.	To garnish the soup: Fry speck lardons in a non-stick pan until golden brown, then add cav vinegar and beurre noisette, and thyme leaves. Place the garnish in the center of the soup.
