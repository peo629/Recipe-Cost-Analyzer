### ✅ Notes for Developers

* **Indexing Recommendations:**

  * `{ company_id: 1, venue_id: 1, role_id: 1 }` → for role/venue lookup
  * `{ work_email: 1 }` (unique) → for authentication
  * `{ permissions: 1 }` → for fast RBAC filtering

* **Password Handling:**
  Always store `password` as a **bcrypt hash**. Never log or transmit plaintext credentials.

* **Date Fields:**
  Use `datetime.datetime.now(datetime.UTC)` for UTC-aware timestamps to avoid deprecation warnings (see your recent diagnostic logs).
